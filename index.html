FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers on sizing, orders and product care. Can't find what you need? Email us anytime.

How do Aerion products fit?

Most pieces are cut for an athletic fit — close to the body without restriction. If you prefer a relaxed fit, size up. Check the size guide in each product for chest, waist and inseam measurements.

When will my order arrive?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Express delivery takes 2–3 days; standard shipping 3–5 days depending on region. You'll receive tracking as soon as your package leaves our warehouse.

Can I return items I've trained in?

Yes — our 30-day performance guarantee covers normal training wear. Items must be washed and free of damage. Final sale items and customized pieces are excluded.

How should I wash technical fabrics?

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle. Skip fabric softener — it reduces moisture-wicking. Hang dry or tumble low. Avoid ironing directly on stretch panels.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to the US, UK, EU, Canada and Türkiye. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout where applicable. More regions coming in 2026.
